Command & Conquer

Command & Conquer is a real-time strategy game series developed by Westwood Studios. The first game, called Command & Conquer: Tiberian Dawn, was released in 1995. It became one of the most influential strategy games and helped popularize the RTS genre.

The story takes place in the near future, where a mysterious substance called Tiberium appears on Earth. This material spreads across the planet and changes the environment. Although it has enormous economic value, it is also extremely dangerous for living organisms.

The discovery of Tiberium causes a global conflict between two major organizations:

- GDI (Global Defense Initiative) - an international military organization supported by the world's major governments. Its main objective is to protect humanity and stop the spread of Tiberium.

- Brotherhood of Nod - a mysterious and powerful organization led by a charismatic leader known as Kane. Nod believes that Tiberium represents the next stage of human evolution and wants to control its development.

Both factions have completely different military units, technologies and strategies. Their conflict is the central element of the Tiberium storyline.

Informations:

Command & Conquer uses classic real-time strategy mechanics. Players have to construct their base, collect resources, develop technologies and create military units.

The main resource in the Tiberium universe is, naturally, Tiberium. Players use special harvesters to collect it and transport it to refineries. The collected material can then be exchanged for money, which is necessary to build structures and train soldiers.

Each faction has its own advantages and disadvantages. GDI usually relies on powerful vehicles and heavy military equipment, while Nod often uses stealth technology, fast attacks and unconventional weapons.

One of the most recognizable GDI units is the Mammoth Tank. It is a heavily armored vehicle equipped with powerful cannons and rockets. It can destroy enemy structures and defeat many different types of military units.

Other games in the series:

The Command & Conquer series contains several games and different storylines. One of the most popular titles is Command & Conquer: Red Alert, which presents an alternative version of history and focuses on a conflict between the Allies and the Soviet Union.

Another important title is Command & Conquer: Generals. Unlike the Tiberium games, it takes place in a modern military setting. Players can control the United States, China or the Global Liberation Army (GLA).

The series also includes Command & Conquer 3: Tiberium Wars and Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3, which introduced improved graphics, additional units and new gameplay mechanics.

Additional information:

Command & Conquer is considered one of the most important real-time strategy franchises in gaming history. Its gameplay, memorable soundtrack, live-action cinematics and interesting campaigns helped it become extremely popular among strategy fans.

Even today, many players remember the series as one of the classics of the RTS genre. Its influence can still be seen in modern strategy games.
