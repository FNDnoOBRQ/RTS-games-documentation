StarCraft 2

StarCraft 2 informations:

StarCraft II is a real-time strategy game developed by Blizzard Entertainment. The game takes place in a science-fiction universe in 25's centuries where three different races — Terran, Protoss, and Zerg — fight for control of the galaxy. Players must collect resources, build bases, train armies, and defeat their opponents using strategic thinking and tactical skills. The game offers a single-player campaign, multiplayer battles, and cooperative missions.


Warcraft III

Warcraft III informations:

Warcraft III is a real-time strategy game developed by Blizzard Entertainment. The game takes place in a fantasy world where different races, including Humans, Orcs, Night Elves, and Undead, fight for power and survival. Players must gather resources, build bases, train armies, and control powerful heroes with unique abilities. The game features a story-driven campaign, multiplayer battles, and strategic gameplay that combines army management with hero development.


Command and conquer

Command & Conquer informations:

Command & Conquer is a real-time strategy game series developed by Westwood Studios and later continued by other studios. The games often focus on conflicts between rival factions fighting for control of resources and territory. Players must collect resources, construct bases, build military units, and use tactical strategies to defeat their enemies. The series is known for its action-packed battles, cinematic cutscenes, and engaging single-player campaigns.
