StarCraft 2

StarCraft 2 - is a real-time-stategy game, made in 2010 by 'Blizzard Entertainment', which also has a prequel with title 'StarCraft Brood war'. StarCraft 2 is about space conflict between 3 races:
-Terrans - dark descendants of humanity like murders, prisoners and others. They were sentenced to colonise planets in unknown space. After many years that they spent on foreign planet they met with second race - "ZERG".
-Zergs - are the simple creatures with counteless amout of units, which help while conquesting others planets. Zerg is one of two races that were created by 'gods'.
-Protoses - the third and final race in StarCraft 2. The are like zergs, created by 'gods'.

Informations:

There are a lots of unique units with different goals. Someone attack ground units, someone flying units and someone even can heal.... On of the most iconic units is Hydralisc.
This unit belong to Zergs, he has fast attack speed, can attack both ground and flying units and is not expensive to create.
